History of PGC

Punjab Colleges has a proud legacy of academic excellence and student achievement. The institution began with a vision to provide quality education and continues to empower students across Pakistan.

Started in 1985 in Lahore, Punjab Colleges has expanded its network across Punjab. With more than 456 campuses in over 216 cities, the institution has always taken pride in delivering quality education. Over the years, students of Punjab Colleges have secured top positions in Pakistan’s national board examinations for intermediate. The Punjab Group of Colleges (PGC) was founded by Mian Amer Mahmood and has grown from a single commerce college into one of the largest private educational networks in Pakistan. The network now includes institutions such as the University of Central Punjab and Allied Schools. Punjab College Chishtian is part of this nationwide network and serves students in Chishtian city, Bahawalnagar district. The campus offers intermediate programs including FSc, FA, ICS, and ICom while focusing on academic excellence, modern facilities, and student development.
