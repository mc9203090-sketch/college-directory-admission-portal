Our Journey

A Legacy of Quality Education

Punjab Colleges has a proud educational legacy built around learning, personal development, and preparing students for their future. Its journey began with a vision to make quality education accessible to students.

Founded by Mian Amer Mahmood, Punjab Colleges began its journey in Lahore in 1985 and expanded its educational network across Punjab and other parts of Pakistan.

The wider educational network associated with the group includes institutions such as the University of Central Punjab and Allied Schools.

At the Chishtian campus, students can explore intermediate study options such as FSc, FA, ICS, and ICom, subject to current campus offerings and admission policies.
