OUR MISSION, VISION AND VALUES

Mission

Arbor School provides an education that connects our community with the natural world and inspires students to flourish while embracing sustainability and ethical stewardship.

Vision

To cultivate a compassionate, resilient, and environmentally conscious community of learners empowered to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Values

We uphold British values in an international context, emphasizing ecological awareness and our commitment to "Enough For All, Forever."
