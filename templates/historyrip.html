BACKGROUND RIPAH

Riphah International College (RIC) Chishtian is a prominent institution committed to academic excellence and modern skill development. The college aims to provide quality education while preparing students for future challenges.

Riphah International College (RIC) Chishtian, located on 13-G Road near Daranwala Mor, is an important campus of the rapidly growing RIC network in Pakistan. The college offers a wide range of intermediate programs including FSc (Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering), ICS, FA/FA IT, and I.Com, along with Associate Degree Programs (ADP) in fields such as Computer Science, IT Management, English, Psychology, Accounting *&* Finance, and Health Sciences (MLT, MIT, OTT).

The institution operates under the leadership of Principal Engr. Khuzama Tahir and focuses on combining traditional academics with modern skills. Students are also provided with opportunities to learn freelancing, e-commerce, and digital marketing, especially at the intermediate level.

Riphah College Chishtian provides a modern learning environment with well-equipped computer labs and career-oriented training facilities. It actively organizes extracurricular activities such as the Riphah Talent Contest (RTC), engaging a large number of students and promoting their talents.

The college is part of the Riphah International University network (chartered in 2002), which is dedicated to delivering quality education while promoting Islamic ethical values and character building among students.
