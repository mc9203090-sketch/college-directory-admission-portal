BACKGROUND RIPAH

Riphah International College (RIC) Chishtian is an educational institution focused on academic learning, skills development, and students' personal growth.

Riphah International College (RIC) Chishtian is located on 13-G Road, near Daranwala Mor, Chishtian. It is part of the Riphah educational network in Pakistan and aims to provide students with educational opportunities and a supportive learning environment.

The college offers intermediate programs such as FSc Pre-Medical, FSc Pre-Engineering, ICS, FA/FA IT, and I.Com. It also offers Associate Degree Programs in different fields. Students should contact the college to confirm the programs available for the current academic session.

The institution aims to combine academic education with modern skills. Depending on the available programs and activities, students may have opportunities to explore technology, freelancing, e-commerce, and digital marketing.

Riphah College Chishtian also aims to provide a learning environment that supports students' academic and personal development. Computer facilities and extracurricular activities can help students develop practical skills, confidence, and teamwork.

The wider Riphah network emphasizes education alongside ethical values and character development. Students are encouraged to pursue academic progress while developing responsibility, respect, and positive social values.

Our Aim

To support students through quality education, practical learning opportunities, and personal development so they can prepare for future academic and professional goals.
