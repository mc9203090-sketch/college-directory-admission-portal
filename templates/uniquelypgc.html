Uniquely Punjab College

What makes Punjab College Chishtian special? We encourage students to develop confidence, creativity, and a passion for learning. We challenge students to think beyond limits and strive for excellence in their academic and personal development.

We welcome different perspectives and believe that learning can help bring positive change to our campus, city, and country. Our aim is to support students through quality education, responsible conduct, and strong values.

We build bright futures every day.
In ways big and small.

This is who we are—and this is what makes the PGC experience distinctive.
