ABOUT US

Know Us

Established in 1985, the College of the Punjab is the largest and the oldest seat of higher learning. With 456 campuses in 216+ cities, Punjab Colleges has always taken pride in the quality of education it delivers.

Over the years, our students have held top positions in Pakistan’s national board examinations for intermediate.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide quality education to ensure that our graduates are nurtured to be sensitive and tolerant. This will hone their potential for assuming leadership roles in diverse fields of the society.

Our Vision

Develop scientific, socio-cultural, economic and political leadership, through learner-centred teaching and research among the graduates in order to promote national cohesion and stability through providing quality education at par with international standards.

CAMPUS LIFE

Clubs & Societies:

Apart from academics, Punjab Colleges provide extra classroom activities to their students in the form of societies & clubs for Dramatics, Music, Debating, literary, IT, sports and more.

Annual Sports Gala:

At Punjab Colleges, a mega sports event is organized annually to encourage extra-curricular and sports activities. Our students participate in local, regional and nation-wide competitions.

Study & Recreational Trips:

Punjab Colleges offer vibrant student life with its recreational activities through trips to movie cinemas, seminars and adventure tours.

Welcome & Farewell Events:

From the time our students join us till the time they leave, Punjab Colleges arrange a celebration of the memorable moments of fun and joy with their students each year.
