Historical Background of Superior

Sir Asif Iqbal, Director

Under the inspiring leadership of Sir Asif Iqbal, our institution is committed to academic excellence, character building, and holistic student development.

Superior College Chishtian, part of the extensive Superior Group of Colleges founded by Prof. Dr. Ch. Abdul Rehman in 2000, is a prominent educational institute located on Bahawalnagar Road, Chishtian. Superior College Chishtian is located at 2-km Bahawalnagar Road, Chishtian Mandi, Punjab, Pakistan. Superior Group of Colleges Chishtian offers Intermediate programs including FSc, ICS, FA along with professional diplomas.

Particularly for girls, this college is an important educational opportunity because many degree programs match students' educational aspirations and future goals. The original page describes 2019 as a planned period of growth for Superior, including additional departments, degree programs, faculty and students.

The campus provides scholarship opportunities, transport facilities, and academic support.
