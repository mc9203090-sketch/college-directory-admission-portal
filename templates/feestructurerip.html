FEE STRUCTURE

We are committed to supporting students in pursuing their educational goals. Scholarships and fee concessions can help eligible students access educational opportunities and plan for their future.

SCHOLARSHIPS & FEE CONCESSIONS

At Riphah College, scholarships and fee concessions are intended to encourage academic achievement and support students who need financial assistance. Eligible students may apply under the college's applicable scholarship and concession categories. The availability, amount, and conditions of each concession should be confirmed with the college administration.

PROCEDURE

1. Merit-Based Scholarship

Students with strong academic performance may be eligible for merit-based scholarships. The required marks and fee waiver depend on the college's current policy.

2. Teachers' Children

Eligible children of teachers may apply for a fee concession. Applicants should confirm the eligibility criteria and required documents with the college administration.

3. Kinship Concession

Students who have eligible family members studying at, or previously associated with, the institution may ask the administration whether a family concession is available.

4. Orphan Students

Students who have lost a parent or guardian may contact the college to ask about available financial assistance and the documents needed to apply.

5. Students with Special Needs

Students with special needs can contact the administration to discuss available support and any applicable fee concession arrangements.

6. Need-Based Concession

Students facing financial difficulties may ask about need-based support. The college may require relevant documents to assess an application.

7. Rescue 1122 Employees' Children

Children of eligible Rescue 1122 employees may contact the college to confirm whether a special concession applies to their admission.

8. Shaheed Personnel's Children

Families of personnel who lost their lives in service may contact the college to enquire about applicable educational assistance and eligibility requirements.

9. Incentives for High Achievers

Students with outstanding board examination results may enquire about academic awards or incentives available under the college's current policy.

10. Matriculation High Achievers

Students who achieve excellent matriculation results can ask the admissions office about any incentives offered to eligible new students.

11. University Admission Incentives

Students planning to continue their education at university level should contact the relevant institution to confirm whether alumni concessions or other incentives are available.

12. Financial Assistance in Special Circumstances

If a student's family experiences a serious change in circumstances, such as the loss of a parent or guardian, the family can contact the college to discuss possible financial support.

Other Discounts

Students interested in afternoon programs should ask the admissions office whether any special fee discounts apply to their chosen program.

Important Note

Scholarship percentages, eligibility conditions, attendance requirements, and application deadlines must be confirmed directly with Riphah College Chishtian. The information above is a general guide, not confirmation of current fee policy.
