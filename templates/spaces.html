Facilities at Superior College

Superior College, Chishtian provides a learning environment where students can develop their knowledge, practical skills and confidence. Our facilities support classroom learning and independent study.

Biology Laboratory

The biology laboratory supports practical learning. Students can explore biological concepts and connect classroom lessons with laboratory activities.

Library

The library provides a space for reading, research and independent study. Students can use learning resources to improve their knowledge and develop good study habits.

Computer Laboratory

The computer laboratory supports computer-based learning and practical activities. It helps students develop their understanding of computer technology.
