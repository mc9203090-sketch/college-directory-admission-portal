Activities

Discover opportunities to explore your interests, develop your abilities, work with others and participate in college life. At Chishtian Science College, learning extends beyond the classroom.

Welcome to a place where community is not just a word but a way of life. Explore opportunities to discover your interests, develop your talents, connect with others and make a positive contribution.

DEBATING SOCIETY

The Debating Society helps students explore their abilities in debating and declamation. Bilingual debating events give students opportunities to express their ideas, improve public speaking, build confidence and participate in debating competitions.

DRAMATIC CLUB

Dramas, short plays and skits help students express themselves through performing arts. Participation can improve confidence, communication, teamwork and personality development.

MUSIC SOCIETY

The Music Society provides opportunities for students interested in singing and music to develop their abilities through practice, workshops, singing competitions and musical performances.

LITERARY CIRCLE

The Literary Circle encourages healthy reading and writing habits. Book discussions, poetry readings and literary sessions can help students improve their expression and appreciation of Urdu and English literature.

PHOTOGRAPHY CLUB

The Photography Club provides a space for students interested in photography to develop their creative skills and learn about capturing and presenting images.

SPORTS CLUB

Sports help students develop teamwork, discipline and a healthy competitive spirit. Activities may include badminton, basketball, cricket, hockey, football and table tennis, subject to college arrangements.

HEALTH AWARENESS CLUB

Health-awareness activities can help students learn about important health issues, healthy habits and wellbeing through educational sessions and awareness events.

GO GREEN CLUB

The Go Green Club encourages environmental responsibility through tree planting, recycling, energy conservation and the careful use of natural resources.

SCIENCE CLUB

The Science Club encourages curiosity through science-related activities, practical projects, educational visits and events that help students explore scientific ideas.

ART CLUB

The Art Club gives students opportunities to develop and showcase their creative abilities through artwork, art competitions and exhibitions.

ISLAMIC SOCIETY

The Islamic Society provides opportunities for students to learn about Islamic values and participate in educational sessions, discussions and Qira'at competitions.
