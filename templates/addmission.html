Welcome to Superior Admissions. We are delighted that you are considering enrolling your child at our college.

Choosing a college is a big decision, and it’s natural to feel a little overwhelmed by the options. Our role is to make that process feel clear, supportive and unhurried, so you can make the choice that feels right for your child and family.

Our College is truly unique; from the way we teach and care for our students to the living campus they learn at every day. The best place to start your journey is with a conversation. We’re always happy to talk through what matters most to you, answer your questions honestly, and help you understand whether Superior could be the right fit.

If visiting in person isn’t possible at first, we’re here to support you remotely, with call-backs and one-to-one guidance throughout the admissions process. Our team will work closely with you to make the transition as smooth and reassuring as possible for your child.

Whatever stage you’re at, we’d love to help. We look forward to welcoming you into the Superior community.
