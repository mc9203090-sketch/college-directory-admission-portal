Scholarships & Fee Concessions

Explore possible financial support options for your education at Punjab College Chishtian.

We’re here to support your future success and make quality education more accessible. Our aim is to help students and families explore financial support options that may make their educational goals easier to achieve.

SCHOLARSHIPS & FEE CONCESSIONS

Punjab Colleges encourages academic achievement and aims to support students who need financial assistance. Scholarships and concessions may be offered under different categories, subject to the college's applicable policies, eligibility criteria, and approval. Contact the campus administration to confirm current terms and availability.

Scholarship Categories

1. Merit-Based Scholarship

Students with strong academic results may be eligible for merit-based scholarships or fee waivers. The required marks and concession amount should be confirmed with the campus at the time of admission.

2. Teachers’ Children

Children of eligible teachers may be considered for a fee concession under the applicable college policy. Required documents and eligibility conditions should be confirmed with the administration.

3. Kinship Concession

Eligible family members of current or former students may be considered for a concession. Ask the campus about the required proof of relationship.

4. Orphan Students

Students who have lost a parent or guardian may ask about financial assistance and the documents needed to apply.

5. Students with Special Needs

Students with disabilities or special educational needs can contact the campus to discuss available support and any applicable fee concession.

6. Need-Based Concession

Students facing financial hardship may request an assessment for financial assistance. Approval depends on the applicable policy and supporting documents.

7. Rescue 1122 Employees’ Children

Eligible children of Rescue 1122 employees may ask the administration whether a concession is available and what proof is required.

8. Shuhada’s Children

Families of eligible martyrs from the armed forces or police may contact the college to ask about any applicable educational concession.

9. Incentives for High Achievers

Students with exceptional board examination results may be considered for academic incentives under the applicable policy. Confirm the current awards and eligibility requirements directly with the campus.

10. Matric Position Holders

Students achieving top positions in matriculation examinations may ask about any available incentives when applying for admission.

11. PGC Alumni University Concessions

Former PGC students may inquire about possible concessions at partner universities. Availability depends on the current arrangements and the receiving university's rules.

12. Special Circumstances

If a student's parent or guardian passes away or the family experiences a serious financial emergency, contact the campus administration to ask about possible assistance.

Other Discounts

Some programmes or study shifts may have separate fee arrangements. Ask the campus to confirm whether any discount applies to your chosen programme.

Important Note

Scholarship amounts, eligibility, attendance requirements, and continuation rules can change. Please confirm the latest official policy and required documents with Punjab College Chishtian before relying on a concession.
