ABOUT US

Know Us

Chishtian Science College is a well-known institution recognized for its excellence in intermediate science education. The college has built a strong reputation in the Bahawalpur division, where its students consistently secure top positions. Focused on preparing students for professional fields like engineering and medicine, the college continues to achieve outstanding academic success, including securing 43 board positions in November 2025.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to deliver quality education that nurtures students into responsible, tolerant, and compassionate individuals. We aim to develop their abilities so they can confidently take on leadership roles across various fields of society.

Our Vision

Develop scientific, socio-cultural, economic and political leadership, through learner-centred teaching and research among the graduates in order to promote national cohesion and stability through providing quality education at par with international standards.

CAMPUS LIFE

Clubs *&* Societies:

Apart from academics,Chishtian Science Colleges provide extra classroom activities to their students in the form of societies *&* clubs for Dramatics, Music, Debating, literary, IT, sports and more.

Annual Sports Gala:

At Chishtian Science Colleges, a mega sports event is organized annually to encourage extra-curricular and sports activities. Our students participate in local, regional and nation-wide competitions.

Study *&* Recreational Trips:

Chishtian Science offer vibrant student life with its recreational activities through trips to movie cinemas, seminars and adventure tours.

Welcome *&* Farewell Events:

From the time our students join us till the time they leave, Chishtian Science arrange a celebration of the memorable moments of fun and joy with their students each year.
