Activities

Welcome to a place where community is not just a word but a way of life. At PGC, we believe that every individual has something valuable to contribute. Explore on- and off-campus opportunities to find your people, explore your passions, and make a difference.

DEBATING SOCIETY

The Debating Society plays a prominent role in facilitating students with the possibilities to explore their capability in the field of debating/declamations. The society conducts bilingual monthly and annual debating events. The best teams are presented with trophies on the basis of their performance. The society also selects the teams of debaters for participation in the inter-college debating contests.

DRAMATIC CLUB

At Punjab Colleges, dramas, short plays and skits play an integral part at welcome parties, concerts and other occasions. Through the dramatics club, students gain confidence, improve their public speaking skills and learn to express themselves. This club aims to contribute to personality and character-building through the performing arts.

MUSIC SOCIETY

The Music Society has been established to polish the innate abilities of students in singing and music. Pakistan’s most popular superstars like Atif Aslam, Abrar-ul-Haq, Goher Mumtaz and Mustafa Zahid started their journeys of success through this society. The society organizes regular workshops, singing competitions and concerts.

LITERARY CIRCLE

This society aims to promote healthy reading and writing habits among students. The circle convenes regular events and each session includes book discussions, poetry reading and critiques. Sessions are conducted in both Urdu and English literatures.

PHOTOGRAPHY CLUB

The Photography Club provides a supportive environment for students interested in photography. Workshops with professional photographers and competitions are organized regularly.

SPORTS CLUB

Extracurricular activities are incomplete without sports and here at Punjab College, we keep this on the top of all activities. League tournaments of badminton, basketball, cricket, hockey, football and table tennis are organized regularly.

HEALTH AWARENESS CLUB

Health Awareness Club arranges activities related to general health awareness among students. Seminars are conducted with experienced doctors to increase awareness about important health issues.

GO GREEN CLUB

The Go Green Club focuses on energy conservation, recycling natural resources and planting trees so that coming generations benefit from decisions made today.

SCIENCE CLUB

Science Club is dedicated to providing students with science-oriented activities including field trips, campus projects and science events.

ART CLUB

The Art Club is responsible to streamline the production, promotion and showcasing of artwork by students. Members participate in art competitions and exhibitions.

ISLAMIC SOCIETY

The Islamic Society promotes activities that build a positive image of Islam. Seminars with Islamic scholars and Qira’at competitions are organized regularly.
