Uniquely Chishtian Science

What makes Chishtian Science College... well, special? We encourage students to develop confidence, creativity, and a passion for learning. At Science, we challenge students to think beyond limits and achieve excellence in academics and character.

We welcome and celebrate differences, because we believe great ideas come from diverse perspectives. Our goal is to bring positive change to our campus, our city, and our country through quality education and strong values.

At Chishtian Science College, we continuously strive to improve learning, support research, and inspire students to build, create, discover, and grow every day.

We build bright futures every day.
In ways big and small.

This is who we are. And, this is why we’re always on to something Uniquely CSC.
