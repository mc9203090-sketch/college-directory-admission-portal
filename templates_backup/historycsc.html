History of CSC

Chishtian Science Colleges has a proud legacy of academic excellence and student achievement. The institution began with a vision to provide quality education and continues to empower students across Pakistan.

Established as a leading institution for science education, Chishtian Science College has built a strong reputation for academic excellence in intermediate examinations. The college is widely recognized for its outstanding performance, particularly in the Bahawalpur division, where its students consistently achieve top positions. Catering specifically to science students, the institution prepares learners for higher education in fields such as engineering and medicine. Over the years, it has emerged as a focused and high-achieving private sector college. In November 2025, the college was highlighted for securing 43 board positions, further strengthening its status as a prominent and successful institution in the region. It is important to note that this refers to the private sector Chishtian Science College, distinct from the Government Graduate College Chishtian established in 1962.
